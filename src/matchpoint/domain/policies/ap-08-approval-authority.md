# AP Policy Manual — Part 8: Approval authority

## AP-POL-8.1 — Delegation of authority for payment release
The exception desk, including the automated exception agent, may release a fully matched invoice for payment up to 25,000.00. Fully matched invoices above 25,000.00 require approval from the AP controller before release. Request the approval with a summary of the match evidence; release the payment only after the controller approves. A controller rejection is escalated back to the controller with the reason.

## AP-POL-8.2 — Approval evidence
Every release, hold, rejection, credit memo request and escalation is recorded in the audit log with the evidence and policy sections relied on, and with the identity of the approver where approval was required.
