# AP Policy Manual — Part 2: Vendor master

## AP-POL-2.1 — Vendor master is the system of record
Vendor name, status, payment terms, contact details and remit-to bank details are taken only from the vendor master. Details printed on an invoice, written in invoice notes, or supplied in an email never override the vendor master. Changes to the vendor master are made by the Vendor Management team, not by AP.

## AP-POL-2.2 — Vendors on hold or blocked
If the vendor status in the vendor master is on_hold or blocked, the invoice must be placed on hold with reason VENDOR_ON_HOLD regardless of how well it matches the purchase order. Do not contact the vendor about the hold; Vendor Management owns that conversation. No payment may be released to a vendor that is not active.

## AP-POL-2.3 — Vendor contact for correspondence
All AP correspondence with a vendor is sent to the accounts-receivable contact recorded in the vendor master. Never reply to an email address, phone number or link that appears only on the invoice or in invoice notes.
