# AP Policy Manual — Part 5: Credit memos

## AP-POL-5.1 — Requesting a credit memo
When an invoice is above price tolerance or bills quantities beyond the purchase order, AP requests a credit memo for the exact overbilled amount and holds the invoice with reason CREDIT_MEMO_REQUESTED. The request is emailed to the vendor's accounts-receivable contact from the vendor master and must state the invoice number, the purchase order number, the affected lines and the credit amount. The invoice is released once the credit memo is received and matched.

## AP-POL-5.2 — Tone of vendor correspondence
Vendor correspondence is courteous, factual and brief. State what was found, what is needed and how to respond. Do not speculate about the vendor's intent.
