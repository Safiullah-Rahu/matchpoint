# AP Policy Manual — Part 9: Communications

## AP-POL-9.1 — Vendor email standards
Vendor emails are drafted for review and sent from the shared AP mailbox. Address the vendor's accounts-receivable team, reference the invoice number and purchase order, state the amount concerned, and give one clear request. Keep emails under 200 words.

## AP-POL-9.2 — Information that must not appear in vendor emails
Never include bank account numbers, internal case notes, fraud suspicions, other vendors' information, or details of internal controls and tolerances in vendor correspondence.
